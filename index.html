    Haze in four steps

    Everyday choices have a carbon weight. This shows you the week's, while there's still week left.

    • 1
      Log what you didCar, bus, flight, electricity, meals. Quantity and date, that's all.
    • 2
      Set a weekly targetMonday to Sunday. The chart plots you against it and against an even pace.
    • 3
      Watch the skyIt clears when you're comfortable and hazes over as the week fills up. Going over never blocks anything.
    • 4
      Try a changeThe Insights tab lets you move car trips to the bus, or swap meals, and see what the week would have been.
